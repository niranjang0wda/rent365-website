Billing questions

How do I cancel Pro?+

Cancel anytime in Google Play › Payments & subscriptions › Subscriptions. You keep Pro until the end of the period you paid for.

Is there a free trial?+

When a free trial is available, the Pro screen in the app shows it before you subscribe.

What happens to my data if Pro ends?+

Nothing is deleted. You keep all your records and properties. You just can't add more properties or use Pro features until you upgrade again.

Can I get a refund?+

Refunds are handled by Google Play under its refund policies. Write to us if you need help.
