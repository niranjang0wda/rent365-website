Getting started

How do I add my first property?+

Open Properties and tap +. Enter a name, address, type and the number of units. Then add a tenant to each unit.

Can one house have several tenants?+

Yes. Give the property more than one unit, such as ground floor and first floor, and add a tenant to each.

Do I need an account?+

No. Rent 365 works without sign-up. Your records are stored on your phone.

Rent & bills

What happens if a tenant pays less or more?+

With carry-forward on, a shortfall is added to the next bill and any extra is kept as an advance. Each tenant's statement shows the running balance.

How do I add an electricity bill?+

Open the property, tap Meter readings, enter the current reading for each unit and tap Add to bills. Units and amount are worked out at your rate.

How do I split a repair between tenants?+

When you add a repair or expense, turn on Add to tenants' bill, choose who shares it and how to split it.

WhatsApp & PDFs

How do I send a receipt on WhatsApp?+

Open the receipt and tap Send on WhatsApp. WhatsApp opens your tenant's chat with the PDF attached; tap send.

Why does WhatsApp ask me to choose a chat?+

The tenant has no phone number saved, or the number isn't on WhatsApp. Add or correct the number in the tenant's details.

Are PDFs free?+

Yes. Receipts and invoices are free to save, share and print.

Backup & data

How do I move to a new phone?+

Back up to Google Drive or a file, install Rent 365 on the new phone, then restore from the same backup.

Can the developer see my data?+

No. Records are stored on your phone and in backups you control.

I forgot my app lock PIN+

Unlock with fingerprint if it's on. Otherwise you'll need to clear the app's data, so keep a backup.

Pro & billing

What does Pro include?+

Unlimited properties, offline use, no ads, reports, the yearly income summary, Excel export, rent agreements and automatic Drive backup.

How much does Pro cost?+

₹60 a month or ₹600 a year, billed through Google Play.

How do I restore Pro on a new phone?+

Sign in with the same Google account and tap Restore purchase in More.
