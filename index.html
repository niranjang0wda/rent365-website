Built for owners with more than one house

Houses, flats, villas and duplexes, with each floor or unit rented separately. Everything a landlord tracks in a notebook, without the notebook.

Rent, bills and balances in one place

A monthly ledger shows paid, pending and overdue rent for every tenant. Record UPI, cash, bank or cheque payments in a few taps.

  • Partial payments, late fees and advances that carry to the next bill
  • Electricity and water readings added to each tenant's bill
  • Scheduled rent increases on lease renewal

Split a repair between tenants

Sump cleaning cost ₹1,000? Choose who shares it, split it equally or set your own share, and each tenant's part is added to their next bill automatically.

  • Works for repairs and any expense, like society maintenance
  • Your share and the tenants' share are always shown

Receipts and invoices on WhatsApp

Send a PDF rent receipt the moment rent arrives, or an invoice before the due date. Tenants get a clear bill with every charge listed.

  • PDF receipts and invoices, free
  • Printable A4 To-let posters for vacant units

  • RemindersOverdue rent, upcoming dues, lease endings and invoice reminders.
  • CalendarDue dates, lease ends, repairs and rent increases on one monthly view.

Your records stay on your phone

Rent 365 has no account to create and no server holding your data. Tenant details, rent and payments are stored only on your device, and backups go to places you choose.
