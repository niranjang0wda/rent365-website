Questions owners ask

Is Rent 365 free?+

Yes, for one property with any number of units and tenants. Pro costs ₹60 a month or ₹600 a year for unlimited properties, offline use, reports and more.

Can I send receipts on WhatsApp?+

Yes. Tap Send on a receipt or invoice and WhatsApp opens your tenant's chat with the PDF attached. You just tap send.

Where is my data stored?+

On your phone. You can back up to a file or to your own Google Drive. The developer cannot see your records.

Does it work for houses split into floors?+

Yes. Add a property with several units and give each unit its own tenant, rent and meter.
